IRCTC booking redesign

I wanted to see if booking a train ticket on IRCTC could feel less stressful. I didn't try to redesign the whole app, just the part from searching for a train to having a ticket.

This is a concept project. I'm not connected to IRCTC, and the trains, times and fares in the screens are made up.

The problem

Booking a ticket on the IRCTC app takes nine steps. When I went through it, three things slowed me down: I had to open each class separately to compare them, I typed every passenger's details again, and when a payment failed, nothing told me whether my money was gone.

I also checked the flow against Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics and made the same booking on ixigo and Paytm.

Wireframe

Two changes made the biggest difference. Showing availability for every class right on the results list removed a whole step. Moving the captcha to login took it out of the booking path completely.

What's next

I haven't tested this with users yet. That's the next step: the same tasks on the current app and on my prototype, then comparing time and success.

After that, I'd like to look at Tatkal booking, where speed matters even more. I'd also add waitlist alerts, so people don't have to keep checking their PNR, and support for Hindi and other regional languages.
