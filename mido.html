Mido: a calm companion for daily meditation

Mido is for people who want to meditate but don't know where to start, or keep forgetting. I spoke to six people aged 25 to 40, then designed a dark, low-glare app with gentle reminders, simple onboarding and a short check-in after each session. I also designed the logo, the visual identity and the motion graphics.
