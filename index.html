How I work

The same four stages on every project, sized to the problem. A two-week fix gets a lighter version than a new product.

01

Discover

Interviews, shadowing and support-ticket digging to find where people actually get stuck.

Output: insight map
02

Define

Turn findings into a sharp problem statement and success metrics the whole team agrees on.

Output: brief + metrics
03

Design

Sketch wide, then prototype the two strongest directions in Figma at real fidelity.

Output: prototype
04

Test & ship

Usability sessions every round, then pairing with engineers through launch and the first data.

Output: shipped UI
