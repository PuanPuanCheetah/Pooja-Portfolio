Brandate: AI that turns one sentence into a whole brand

I designed and built Brandate, an AI tool that turns a one-sentence idea into a brand kit: a colour palette, a font pair, mockups, and a 13-page brand book you can download as a PDF.

01

The idea

A brand usually takes a designer, a lot of back and forth, and a guidelines document at the end. Brandate gets someone to a first, usable version in minutes, so they can start with something real and refine it.

Who it's for

Anyone starting something who needs a brand before they have a designer. The start screen suggests a few ideas to begin from:

Meditation appStreetwear labelNeighborhood bakeryAI tool for small business

What you get

Colours with names, roles and contrast checks. A heading font and a body font that belong together. Your brand on products like cups, bags and boxes. A 13-page brand book as a PDF or an image.

02

Research

I started with public data on how small businesses get their first brand, then talked to owners. Four numbers and one interview shaped the product.

What that meant for Brandate

  1. It has to work before launch. People need a brand at the very start, so Brandate begins with one sentence about an idea, not an existing logo or website.
  2. It has to cost little and take minutes. Most owners won't pay a designer, so Brandate builds the whole kit from one sentence, with no design skills needed.
  3. A logo isn't enough. Brands drift when there are no rules, so every kit ends with a brand book that says how to use the colours, type and logo.

Talking to owners

The data told me what was common. I wanted to hear what it actually feels like, so I started interviewing small business owners and asked them to try Brandate.

Interview 01Café owner, South GoaBranded the café with a freelancer, then tried Brandate
What I'm taking forward

The first interview backs up the data: owners want a brand without the cost and risk of hiring someone. It also shows where Brandate falls short. Today it suggests a simple mark and lets you upload your own logo, but this owner expected it to design the logo too. Logo generation is the next feature I'm exploring.

03

Logo, colour & type

A tool that makes brands needs a confident one of its own. Brandate's identity is built like a magazine: a cover story, an issue, bold type and four bright colours that show up across the whole product.

A lowercase wordmark: teal on light pages, lime on dark ones, where teal is too dim. Under the app bar, a four-colour stripe carries the palette on every screen.

The flow

One chat box, then four steps with a progress bar: Palette, Fonts, Visualization and Brand book. Every step can be edited, and AI actions always wear the gradient so it's clear what the AI does and what you do.

Watch it work · 40 seconds

From one sentence to a brand book

  1. Describe. Type one sentence about the brand and press Generate.
  2. Palette. Five colours with roles: primary, secondary, accent, background and text.
  3. Fonts. A heading and body pair, shown as a real headline and paragraph.
  4. Visualization. The logo, product mockups, a website and an illustration in the new brand.
  5. Brand book. Brandate writes 13 pages you can flip through, edit and download.

The example brand, Saltbox, was generated from: “A small coffee shop with fresh cake every morning.”
